## 5. Code content, and why this matters

A barcode or QR code is content **written by a third party**, and whoever prints it controls its
entire contents. A code can carry a link to a fraudulent site, a Wi-Fi network set up to intercept
traffic, or misleading text.

WhyScan **always shows the content before doing anything with it** and never opens a link on its own
— you have to tap it. It also hands the system only known schemes — `http`, `https`, `mailto`,
`tel`, `sms` and `geo` — and rejects any other.

Even so, the final check is yours: **look at where a link goes before you open it.** Whatever happens
on the site or in the app that opens is outside our control and our responsibility.
